01.1 / Product truth

The strategy before the styling.

Portability is not a specification.
It is permission.

The category is crowded with black cylinders and interchangeable feature claims. The opportunity was to make portability emotional: not “easy to carry,” but a product that belongs wherever the story moves next.

01.2 / The object Find the ownable cue

A black speaker.
One line of light.

The orange carry loop and vertical RGB strip became the recognition system: a route marker, a camp signal and a thread that could move across every surface.

Carry loop / movementRGB strip / signalBlack mesh / durability

01.3 / The world

Build an atmosphere
the product can own.

Open terrain creates scale. Ember orange creates warmth. Maps, rope and rugged surfaces turn portability into a lived scene instead of a technical bullet.

01.5 / Packaging The world folded onto the shelf

Not a box around
the campaign.
The campaign as a box.

The packaging carries the same horizon, signal colour, material cues and movement language—so the product remains inside one world from discovery to unboxing.
