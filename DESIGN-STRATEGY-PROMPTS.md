## Shared production rules

- Recommended size for every image: **2400 × 1600 px**.
- Keep all five images in the same 3:2 horizontal ratio.
- Use the supplied WAVEX speaker reference exactly.
- Preserve the matte-black cylindrical body, woven mesh, orange hanging loop, top controls and vertical RGB strip.
- Do not redesign the product.
- No embedded typography, logos, fake feature badges, packaging or extra speakers.
- Use premium advertising photography, realistic materials and controlled black/orange colour grading.
- Keep the product clearly visible in the lower and mid-tone areas.
